---
name: grilling
description: Grill the user relentlessly about a plan or design. Use when the user wants to stress-test a plan before building, or uses any 'grill' trigger phrases.
---

Interview me relentlessly about every aspect of this plan until we reach a shared understanding. Walk down each branch of the design tree, resolving dependencies between decisions one-by-one.

- Look up facts discoverable from the filesystem, code, tools, or documentation rather than asking me for them.
- Ask me to make consequential decisions one at a time. For each decision, provide your recommended answer, then wait for my response before continuing.
- Do not act on the result until I confirm we have reached a shared understanding.
